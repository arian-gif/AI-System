The same chunk of text is fed through the model at every snapshot. Under each character is the model's guess for the character that comes next (green = right, red = wrong). Click a character to follow it through the layers below.

What each layer outputs

One picture per stage: the embedding + position, then the output of each decoder block. Every row is one character of the chunk and every column is one of its numbers (blue = negative, orange = positive). The white box is the character you clicked. Watch how the numbers change from layer to layer.

Attention

One picture per head for the block you pick. Row = the character doing the looking, column = the character it's looking at, brighter = pays more attention. It's a triangle because a character can only look backwards. The white box is the row of the character you clicked.

Embedding space

Each of the characters' embedding vectors squashed down to 2D. At the start they're random, and as training goes similar characters may drift together. Blue = lowercase, orange = uppercase, green = everything else. The circle is the character you clicked.
